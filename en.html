Baby Boss · Israel

Strollers and high chairs, made to be seen.

Baby Boss is an Israeli company that develops its own strollers and high chairs, and imports and distributes selected baby brands across Israel. It was founded in 2013 by Kobi Sofer, the second generation of a family in the Israeli baby market since 1981.

Work with us What we do

Three things we do

  1. 01

    Our own brand

    Four strollers built on one shared base, with the design done together with a Spanish children's fashion house: Bobcat 3, Panther 3, Tiger 3 and Cheetah 3. And three high chairs, Paloma, Slim Eat and Volt, sold by the thousands every year.

  2. 02

    Import

    We bring carefully chosen brands from abroad to Israeli parents, and look for more all the time.

  3. 03

    Distribution

    We sell through our own online store, KSP, the major Israeli marketplaces and baby stores across the country.

Two generations in the baby business

  1. 1981

    Kobi's father opens Baby Center, a chain of baby stores. Kobi starts working there at 13.

  2. 2002

    Kobi and his father found Baby Club, a chain of baby superstores.

  3. 2013

    Kobi founds Baby Boss.

  4. 2015

    Baby Club is sold to the Shilav chain.

  5. 2016

    Work begins on the first Baby Boss stroller.
